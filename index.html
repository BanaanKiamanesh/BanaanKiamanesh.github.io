M.Sc Control Systems Engineering - University of Padova

Profile

I design end-to-end experiments that move from simulation to flight tests: sensor fusion and pose estimation feed into cascaded control stacks; controllers are validated in simulation, bench tests, and on-vehicle trials with attention to safety, repeatability, and clear documentation.

Open-source approach: I develop modular, efficient, and affordable software - MATLAB, C/C++ libraries and drivers designed for low overhead and easy integration. My projects use permissive licensing, concise examples, and automated tests to lower the barrier to entry and enable real-world adoption by hobbyists and practitioners alike.
