Control systems engineer and roboticist

I am a Control Systems Engineer and Roboticist who turns control theory into reliable, field-ready systems. I focus on aerial robotics, intelligent control, multi-agent systems and decentralized cognition.

Profile

I design end-to-end experiments that move from simulation to flight tests: sensor fusion and pose estimation feed into cascaded control stacks; controllers are validated in simulation, bench tests, and on-vehicle trials with attention to safety, repeatability, and clear documentation.

OpenSource approach: I try my best to develop better and better software. I develop modular, efficient, and affordable software - MATLAB, Python, C/C++ libraries and drivers designed for low overhead and easy integration. My projects use permissive licensing, concise examples, and automated tests to lower the barrier to entry and enable real-world adoption by hobbyists and practitioners alike or at least try my best doing it.
