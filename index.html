Profile

I design and implement control systems for aerial robots and robot arms, with a strong emphasis on modeling, simulation, and robust control. My work spans distributed localization, optimization, and intelligent estimation for real-world robotics.

I also build embedded modules and open-source learning resources to support students and researchers in control theory, robotics, and reinforcement learning.
