About

Control systems, robotics, and applied AI.

I am a control systems engineer, programmer, and maker focused on robotics, flight control, and multi-agent systems.

Background

MSc Electrical Engineering (Control) student at the University of Padua. My work centers on robust control, modeling, and simulation for aerial robots and manipulators, with a strong focus on open-source implementation and reproducible results.

Focus areas

  • Flight control and quadcopter dynamics
  • Manipulator modeling and control frameworks
  • Multi-agent robotics and distributed localization
  • Optimization and system identification
  • Neurotechnology and brain-computer interfaces

Teaching

I teach MATLAB data visualization, covering professional plots, 3D visualization, animation, and export workflows for research and presentations.

Methods and tooling

I build models and controllers in MATLAB and Simulink, prototype algorithms in Python, and deploy embedded systems in C++. My work spans PID, sliding-mode control, inverse dynamics, estimation, and optimization for robotic systems.
